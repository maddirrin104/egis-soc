# W1-08-FU bounded literature search and screening log

**Run date and publication cutoff:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** accepted for the bounded W1-08-FU review under [D-015](./context/DECISION_LOG.md). This is a reproducible query-level follow-up to the [approved W1-08 v0.3 matrix](./literature-matrix.md), not an exhaustive systematic review or a replacement for R2's search. No EGIS experiment, held-out item or dataset was accessed.

## Search protocol actually used

The search platform was the connected **web search index** (`system2_search_query`), followed by direct reading of primary venue, publisher, institutional or arXiv pages with the web opener. The index can change ranking and coverage; no native IEEE Xplore, ACM DL, Scopus or Web of Science account/export was used. Searches were run on **2026-10-18** without domain or recency filters, then screened to publications available by that date. R2's references [26], [47], [49] and [81] were also chased from its [complete 25-page article](https://www.mdpi.com/2624-800X/5/4/95/pdf), pp. 21–23. The official PDF and the copy used in the initial follow-up were verified byte-identical on this date; see the [R2 audit](./literature-matrix.md). R1 was already in the core matrix and was not counted again. Search hits are candidate leads, not verification of results. The exact queries and run date are reproducible inputs; search-index ranking and the unexported full hit set are not reproducible outputs.

**Include:** English research with an accessible primary record, a clear publication/preprint date by the cutoff, and direct relevance to at least one of: grounded SOC incident reporting, security incident analysis, claim/citation support, evidence-based revision, omission/coverage, analyst review or genuine environment shift. Prefer peer-reviewed USENIX/IEEE/ACM/ACL-family and established journals; preprints remain labelled. **Exclude from the comparison:** generic threat detection or autonomous remediation without report/evidence evaluation; log parsing alone; duplicate preprint/venue versions; secondary blogs and untraceable results; later publications. An abstract-only candidate can remain in the screening queue but supplies no detailed methodological finding. The review deliberately selected 12 high-relevance candidates for individual screening; it does not claim 12 were all results returned by the search engine.
