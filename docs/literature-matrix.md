## Research interpretation and open gates

The [source plan §2.1](./EGIS_SOC_Ke_hoach_8_tuan.md) already warns that RAG or citations alone cannot support a novelty claim. R3 establishes **prior SOC multi-agent narrative generation with claim-to-evidence ID binding and citation-chain reporting**, while R4 establishes **SOC atomic Boolean evidence checking, manual citation-plus-support assessment and cross-dataset testing**; R6 establishes three-way attribution verification, and R1/R5 provide relevant summarization, retrieval and coverage measurements. These are direct overlaps, not gaps to ignore. R3's reported 100% citation coverage is **not** a 100% semantic support rate, and its 0.1% no-link “hallucination” metric is **not** EGIS's False Support Rate. This matrix treats C1/C2/C3 as **prespecified hypotheses to test under their exact EGIS comparators**, not established firsts or proof that prior work lacks a component. R2 full text and supplementary candidates remain review items. Retain overlapping and negative prior work as the search expands.

The [W1-05 source assessment](./datasets/source-license-matrix.md) and [Environment B plan](./datasets/environment-b-plan.md) are accepted as documentation only. Neither they nor the [W1-07 Week 1 policy/synthetic acceptance](./split-freeze-policy.md) establish acquired incidents, source rights, an independent/adequate B, frozen splits, completed annotation, W5 drafts, W7 locks or empirical findings. Literature verification cannot substitute for those gates. The project owner accepted **this matrix v0.3 as Week 1 documentation only** under [D-014](./context/DECISION_LOG.md); R2 full-text review and broader related-work verification remain open. No novelty or empirical performance claim is approved.
