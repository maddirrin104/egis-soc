# EGIS-SOC research protocol — approved methodological specification

**Status:** APPROVED W1-02 methodological specification by the project owner on 2026-10-13. **Protocol version:** 0.2 (approved amendment to the 2026-10-12 draft). A-01 through A-04 are approved with the A-03 conditions in Sections 8 and 10; approval does not establish empirical performance, CI validity, operational safety, or final-test readiness. No split, threshold, test prompt, or evaluation fixture is frozen by this document. No experiment or CI calibration has been run for this protocol.

**Authority and traceability:** [8-week source plan](./EGIS_SOC_Ke_hoach_8_tuan.md), [project contract](../AGENTS.md), [research guardrails](./context/RESEARCH_GUARDRAILS.md), [W1-02 task](./context/TASK_BOARD.md), and [approval record](./context/DECISION_LOG.md). The source plan supplies research intent; this specification records the approved methodology and identifies later implementation and final-test locks. A change to a frozen evaluation surface requires a versioned amendment and human approval.

## 1. Research scope and contributions

The input is an already scoped or triaged incident bundle with alerts, relevant telemetry, provenance, and an incident boundary. The output under study is a structured analyst-reviewable incident report containing atomic factual claims, evidence citations, verification status, uncertainty, and critical-fact coverage checks. Incident detection from an unbounded stream, attribution without evidence, autonomous remediation, and production SOC deployment are outside scope. Retrieval must stay within incident and tenant boundaries; source events and provenance remain immutable.

The following are **planned contributions to test**, not established novelty or positive findings:

| ID | Planned contribution | Evidence required to substantiate it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | SOC-specific hybrid verification combining deterministic citation, entity, temporal, and event constraints with pretrained NLI and traceable abstention. | Matched V3-versus-V2 results, error analysis, rule ablations, and independent gold S/C/I labels. |
| C2 | Omission-aware summarization with an operational critical-fact detector and evidence-constrained repair. | Matched S3-versus-S2 CFR, UCR, and CSA results using independent evaluation gold facts; analysis of repair-induced errors. |
| C3 | Evaluation under a genuinely different telemetry environment. | Documented Environment B independence, leakage audit, absolute and relative OOD scores, and separate perturbation results. |

Literature comparison and a claim of novelty require a separately verified literature review. Negative or inconclusive comparisons remain reportable results.

## 2. Research questions and hypotheses

All hypotheses below describe **planned tests**, not findings. Each approved hierarchy is a conjunctive decision rule: the first endpoint is tested first, then each remaining required endpoint. A full RQ claim requires every listed gate; a favorable first endpoint alone may be reported only as a limited component result. RQ3 FSR is a mandatory safety diagnostic, not a confirmatory non-inferiority gate. The confidence-bound method is in Section 8. These rules have methodological approval but still require the later pre-test locks and validation gates in Section 10. Failure to cross a bound means *failure to support*, not equivalence or proof of harm. Undefined required endpoints or failed independence audits preclude a confirmatory conclusion.

### RQ1 — Does hybrid verification improve claim-level factual correctness?

- **Testable hypothesis H1:** On the same held-out claim–evidence pairs extracted from frozen, arm-independent LLM incident drafts, V3 improves three-class Macro-F1 and Supported Precision and reduces False Support Rate relative to V2, as anticipated by the source plan.
- **Null for the full claim:** at least one of `Delta F1 = F1(V3) - F1(V2) <= 0`, `Delta SP = SP(V3) - SP(V2) <= 0`, or `Delta FSR = FSR(V3) - FSR(V2) >= 0` holds.
- **Independent variable:** verifier arm, principally V2 versus V3; V0 and V1 are contextual baselines.
- **Dependent variables:** claim-level S/C/I predictions and their Macro-F1, Supported Precision, False Support Rate, confusion matrix, and abstention/review-routing frequency.
- **Approved endpoint hierarchy:** (1) lower 95% bound for `Delta F1` > 0; (2) lower 95% bound for `Delta SP` > 0; (3) upper 95% bound for `Delta FSR` < 0. Stop confirmatory testing at the first failed or undefined gate. Per-class F1, rule coverage, and multi-evidence errors remain descriptive.
- **Interpretation:** all three gates support the full H1; gate 1 alone supports only a Macro-F1 component result, with any safety limitation stated. Failure or undefinedness at gate 1 means H1 is unsupported. A point estimate in the predicted direction without its required confidence bound is not a passed gate. Report every endpoint and review-routing rate regardless of stopping.

### RQ2 — Does omission checking improve critical-fact coverage safely?

- **Testable hypothesis H1:** S3 increases Critical Fact Recall relative to S2 while its absolute **pooled Unsupported Claim Rate difference** stays below the approved methodological non-inferiority margin delta = 0.03. This three-percentage-point tolerance applies to the difference between pooled rates, not to the number of unsupported assertions; it is a prespecified research-design tolerance, not an empirically established harmless difference or a final-test lock.
- **Null H0:** Delta CFR = CFR(S3) - CFR(S2) <= 0 **or** Delta UCR = UCR(S3) - UCR(S2) >= delta. Both conditions must be addressed; a nonsignificant UCR difference does not establish non-inferiority.
- **Independent variable:** omission checking plus controlled repair (S3), compared with the same verification-only pipeline (S2).
- **Dependent variables:** fact coverage, unsupported claims, citation support, summary length, repair actions, tokens, and latency.
- **Primary comparison and outcomes:** S3 versus S2 on the same held-out incidents; Delta CFR and an upper confidence bound for Delta UCR. CSA is a key secondary safety outcome; word/token count, latency, and analyst corrections are secondary.
- **Approved joint rule:** the lower one-sided 95% bound for `Delta CFR` must exceed 0 **and** the upper one-sided 95% bound for `Delta UCR` must be strictly below approved delta. This is an intersection–union claim; neither a nonsignificant UCR difference nor a favorable UCR point estimate establishes non-inferiority. An undefined bound or either failed gate makes the full H1 unsupported. Report newly introduced unsupported claims and length/citation changes even if both gates pass.

### RQ3 — Does the hybrid verifier retain performance across environments?

- **Testable hypothesis H1 (relative robustness only; approved methodology):** On an independently sourced Environment B, V3 has a smaller A-to-B drop than V2 in both Macro-F1 and Supported Precision. This hypothesis does not assert FSR superiority, FSR non-inferiority, or adequate absolute B performance.
- **Null for the full relative-robustness claim:** `A_F1 <= 0` **or** `A_SP <= 0`, where each drop advantage subtracts V3's A-to-B drop from V2's.
- **Independent variables:** verifier arm (V2/V3) and environment (held-out Environment A versus independent Environment B); shift type is recorded, not inferred from the label alone.
- **Dependent variables:** each arm's absolute B Macro-F1, Supported Precision and FSR; each arm's F1/SP drop; `A_F1` and `A_SP`; false-support counts and denominators; and per-source/family errors.
- **Approved joint decision rule:** (1) the lower endpoint of the two-sided 95% confidence interval for `A_F1` must exceed 0; then (2) the lower endpoint of the two-sided 95% confidence interval for `A_SP` must exceed 0. Both gates and the independent B audit are required for the relative-robustness claim. Stop confirmatory testing at the first failed or undefined gate, but report both advantages and all diagnostics. V2/V3 are paired on claim pairs within each environment; A and B incidents are distinct and are not paired with each other. No FSR gate is part of this claim.
- **Interpretation and safety:** a passed first gate alone is only a relative Macro-F1 component result. A failed independence audit or undefined required endpoint bars a confirmatory RQ3 claim. Always report absolute B Macro-F1, SP, FSR, FSR numerators/denominators, uncertainty, class prevalence, and review routing. If observed B FSR is higher for V3, explicitly qualify any supported relative-robustness conclusion with that safety trade-off; do not call V3 globally safer or operationally ready. Zero observed false-support events or a degenerate interval is uninformative about FSR non-inferiority. A separate FSR superiority/non-inferiority claim or absolute operational floor would require its own defensible pre-test inferential procedure and human approval; none is asserted here. A smaller drop can arise from low A performance and does **not** establish adequate absolute B performance.

## 3. E1 — Evidence verification

The evaluation unit is a human-labeled `(atomic claim, permitted evidence set)` associated with one incident. Ground truth has **exactly three labels**: **Supported** only when the permitted evidence supports every factual atom; **Contradicted** when it directly refutes an atom and no other unresolved atom prevents an unambiguous whole-claim verdict; **Insufficient** when neither full support nor an unambiguous direct contradiction is established. Missing evidence alone is not contradiction. Mixed support/refutation or compound claims must first be atomized; unresolved ambiguity receives human adjudication, not an automatic Contradicted label. W1-04 must lock examples, atomization, precedence, and adjudication before labels are frozen.

`Needs Review` is a separate routing/abstention flag, never a fourth gold label. **Proposed scoring mapping:** a low-confidence, missing-evidence, or unresolved rule/NLI disagreement outcome emits predicted Insufficient plus `review_required=true`; a high-confidence direct contradiction retains predicted Contradicted even if flagged for review. All three-class predictions, including routed and failed cases, remain in the fixed denominator. W1-04 and validation data must fix triggers and thresholds before test; report review rate, prediction distribution and per-class errors on routed versus unrouted pairs so abstention cannot masquerade as accuracy.

| Arm | Method | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V0 | Deterministic rules only | Measures rule coverage and limitations. |
| V1 | TF-IDF plus Logistic Regression trained only on allowed train incidents | Conventional supervised ML baseline. |
| V2 | Pretrained NLI only | Semantic baseline in the primary comparison. |
| V3 | V0-style rules plus the **same pretrained NLI checkpoint as V2**, with versioned fusion policy | Main hybrid treatment. |

V4/V5 fine-tuned arms in the source plan are optional, separately labeled exploratory analyses, never replacements for V2/V3.

**Primary fixed test-pair population (approved methodology; manifest pending).** For RQ1, and under the same procedure separately in A and B for RQ3, sample atomic factual claims from LLM-generated incident drafts for independently selected held-out incidents. Before any V2/V3 inference, fix one arm-independent draft-generation checkpoint/revision, prompt, decoding policy, context budget, retrieval/access policy, and seed policy using train/validation only. Generate drafts from permitted incident evidence without V2/V3 predictions, rule traces, failures, or feedback; freeze the drafts, their evidence snapshots/IDs, and a claim–evidence pairing rule that gives both verifier arms the same serialized permitted evidence. Human annotators, blinded to arm outputs, atomize factual assertions and adjudicate S/C/I against that fixed evidence under the W1-04 rubric. Include all eligible draft claims up to a predeclared per-incident cap; if more exist, use a recorded seed and probability sample independent of verifier behavior. Before test scoring, checksum a manifest recording all eligible candidates, inclusion probabilities, draft/prompt and evidence snapshot IDs, claim/evidence IDs, lineage, gold labels, and exclusions. Do not synthesize test negatives, rebalance labels, select on V2/V3 errors, or tune generation, retrieval, thresholds, prompts, or pairing after inspecting test/OOD results. Report class/incident distributions and use the identical manifest for V2/V3. If a required class is absent, Macro-F1 is `NA` under Section 6 rather than backfilled with synthetic claims.

**Other claim populations and later artifacts.** Source-derived assertions from alerts, source narratives, or pre-existing notes form a separately labeled pilot/diagnostic population and cannot be pooled into the primary draft-claim estimand. Realistic repair-generated claims may form a separate predeclared diagnostic population only when a verifier-blind repair-generation procedure, fixed from train/validation, produces and freezes claims and evidence before either V2 or V3 is run; apply the same independent annotation and manifest rules. Claims produced by the actual S3 policy in response to V3 verdicts are selected by that verifier and belong to E2 downstream output evaluation, not a confirmatory paired V2/V3 RQ1 set. W1-04 owns the claim/annotation rubric; W1-07 owns incident-lineage isolation and frozen pair manifests; W5 produces and archives arm-independent drafts and any verifier-blind repair diagnostics; W7 validates manifests and evaluates the locked test. These artifacts and the LLM need not exist in W1-02. The cap, seed policy, generator configuration, and pairing rule require approval and a pre-test lock.

**Comparison controls.** Run V2 and V3 on the same ordered test pair IDs, exactly the same evidence IDs and serialized evidence representation, and the same NLI checkpoint/revision. Record any failed or missing inference for both arms. Tune all NLI thresholds and the V3 fusion/rule policy using train/validation data only; freeze them before final test. Shared NLI thresholds must remain identical across V2 and V3. V0 rules and V3 rules use the same rule definitions, with V3's fusion decision trace recorded. Report the number and type of pairs on which each rule can act, alongside false rule triggers and cases routed to review.

Multi-evidence claims must be assessed against the **permitted combination** of evidence, not a single convenient event. Claim atomization, evidence ordering/truncation, and maximum context length are versioned. No arm may retrieve extra test evidence or cross incident/tenant boundaries after the comparison input is fixed. Citation ID validity is checked independently of whether the cited content supports the claim.

**Ablations and error analysis.** Predefine V3 variants removing entity rules, temporal rules, event-semantic rules, and abstention/review routing one group at a time. Treat these as secondary; report false Supported predictions, multi-evidence failures, S/C/I confusion, disagreement between rules and NLI, and in-domain versus OOD rule coverage. Do not tune an ablation after reading test errors and then report it as confirmatory.

## 4. E2 — Omission-aware summarization

| Arm | Pipeline |
| --- | --- |
| S0 | LLM-only from an incident context within a predeclared budget. |
| S1 | Structured/BM25 retrieval (RAG), LLM draft, and evidence citations. |
| S2 | S1 plus V3 hybrid claim verification and evidence-based removal, qualification, or correction of unsupported claims. |
| S3 | S2 plus operational critical-fact omission checking, controlled repair, and re-verification of every changed or added claim. |

The **primary contrast is S3 versus S2**, not S3 versus S0 alone. For each incident, run retrieval, initial generation, and S2 verification/repair **once**, archive their evidence IDs, draft, verification trace, and S2 final report with hashes, then fork S2 and S3 from that identical archived S2 state. S2 terminates there; S3 alone executes omission checking and any subsequent controlled repair. Both therefore share incident IDs, initial evidence, retrieval index/configuration, LLM checkpoint/revision, decoding parameters, initial prompt/template, context budget, and per-call input/output token limits. Seeds and stochastic settings are logged but do not substitute for the identical artifact. S3's extra calls are the treatment; record their tokens, latency, and calls. Version and fix stage prompts, repair policy, and a maximum of two additional repair iterations before test. A zero-round outcome is allowed. Stop when no eligible uncovered fact or unsupported claim remains, the cap is reached, or support is insufficient; route unresolved cases to analyst review. If the archived fork state is missing or differs, that incident cannot enter a confirmatory paired S2/S3 comparison; report it as a protocol deviation under the pre-test missing-output policy.

**Gold independence and leakage boundary.** Human annotators construct evaluation `gold_critical_facts` from incident timelines and source evidence without access to generated drafts, arm identities, predictions, or the operational inventory. Record source event IDs, annotator/adjudicator IDs, annotation time, blinding audit, disagreements, and fact provenance. An inference-time *operational fact inventory* may be extracted only from evidence and metadata available under the same access policy to both S2 and S3; S2 does not use the inventory, because omission checking is the treatment. It must not expose held-out evaluation gold facts, IDs, labels, or adjudications to S3. Separate storage and an access audit must demonstrate this boundary before scoring. The operational inventory may be used by S3's omission detector; the independent gold inventory is used only for scoring. A fact is eligible for CFR only when human adjudication finds it critical, supported, and observable in the permitted evidence. The criticality, observability, and semantic-match rubrics remain W1-04 approval gates.

Repair may remove, qualify, or rewrite a failed claim, add an evidence-supported missing fact, or abstain. It may not fabricate evidence or elevate an Insufficient fact to Supported. Re-verify changed claims and citations after every round. Alongside UCR, report per-incident unsupported-claim counts, the number of unsupported claims introduced after the S2 fork (using a locked claim-identity/matching rubric), factual-claim and token counts, uncited claims, and citation counts. These are mandatory dilution diagnostics: a lower pooled UCR does not erase a rise in absolute unsupported assertions or make the RQ2 safety claim unqualified.

## 5. E3 — Cross-environment generalization

**In-domain:** held-out Environment A incidents not sharing an original incident, simulation run, or template lineage with training/validation. **OOD:** Environment B must originate from an independently operated collection or telemetry pipeline, not an A snapshot transformed by renaming, schema remapping, noise, or replay alone. W1-05 must name the candidate source, license, acquisition/collection operator and dates, sensors/instrumentation, schema, log-source mix, simulation/scenario provenance, and original-event lineage. W1-07 must audit incident, run, template, collection, and source-family overlap against every A partition. A meaningful documented shift and absence of test-to-train lineage are necessary; merely assigning an `environment=B` field is not evidence. No B incidents, variants, labels, or gold-derived prompts may enter training, rule/threshold tuning, retrieval tuning, or model selection. If B is unavailable, contaminated, or inadequately documented, report perturbation/feasibility results only and make **no confirmatory RQ3 claim**. Both task gates require documented independent review before final-test access. As an execution-order adjustment to the source plan's W6 OOD-evaluation slot (16–22/11), W6 is limited to B preparation, environment-independence and lineage audits, system profiling, and development experiments on train/validation or designated pilot data. Do not access held-out B results or use final test/OOD data for tuning, calibration, preliminary evaluation, or method selection before the W7 gates.

Report both absolute performance in B and the A-to-B drop for V2 and V3, plus errors by source/family. A hostname or IP replacement within A is **perturbation robustness only**, not proof of a new environment. Preserve original evidence/provenance and record each transformation and its derived-lineage ID.

| Perturbation | Change | Label policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Substitute IPs/hostnames while preserving relationships. | Validate label preservation; do not claim environmental independence. |
| R2 | Remap field names/schema. | Validate that factual meaning and evidence scope remain intact. |
| R3 | Change timestamp/time-zone representation. | Preserve chronology; audit conversion errors. |
| R4 | Add benign events/noise. | Ensure the added context does not change the supported fact set. |
| R5 | Remove selected telemetry. | Human-review and **relabel against remaining observable evidence** whenever support changes; never reuse an invalid original label. |
| R6 | Change log-source mix. | Audit observability and relabel if the available evidence changes support. |

Perturbation variants inherit the original incident's split group and can never cross into a different train/test partition. R1–R6 are secondary stress tests, reported separately from independently sourced OOD performance.

## 6. Metric specification

**Populations and common aggregation policy (approved methodology; implementation pending).** Let `P` be the fixed, human-labeled claim–evidence pairs drawn from frozen arm-independent LLM drafts for E1/E3 under Section 3, with one S/C/I prediction per arm and pair. Source-derived and verifier-blind repair-generated diagnostics have separate manifests and do not enter `P`. Let `C_a` be the set of human-adjudicated atomic factual claims emitted by summary arm `a` over the fixed E2 test incidents; uncited factual claims remain in `C_a`. Let `F` be independently annotated eligible gold critical supported facts. Primary point estimates pool eligible numerator and denominator counts across incidents (*micro* aggregation); for Macro-F1, build one pooled three-class confusion matrix and average its three class F1 values. Inference uncertainty is clustered by independent lineage component under Section 8, not by treating claims, facts, or related incidents as independent. Also publish per-incident and per-environment denominators and distributions. This aggregation rule must be locked and audited before test.

An **atomic factual claim** asserts an observable event, entity, time, behavior, outcome, or impact that can be assessed against evidence. The annotation guideline must fix segmentation and the distinction between a factual assertion and a truthful uncertainty statement before scoring. A clear statement such as “the available evidence does not establish exfiltration” is not an unsupported factual assertion solely because exfiltration is unproven. Every claim that *does* assert exfiltration without support is assessed and counted. Gold support labels are adjudicated from the permitted incident evidence independently of model outputs. Citation validity and factual support are distinct measurements.

**Common undefined and missing-output policy (approved methodology; implementation pending):** never replace a zero-denominator metric with zero or one. Mark it `NA`, report the zero denominator and affected incidents/strata, and exclude it from secondary arithmetic averages. A primary comparison with any `NA` pooled endpoint or confidence bound is not confirmatory. The manifest fixes attempted incidents/pairs before inference. For E1/E3, a missing, timed-out, or unparseable arm prediction is scored as predicted Insufficient with `review_required=true`, tagged as a failure, and retained in `P`; report failure rates by arm so this operational fallback is visible. Any such failure bars the **full confirmatory** RQ1/RQ3 claim, though fixed-denominator descriptive scores remain reportable. For E2, a valid empty report covers zero eligible facts, has per-incident UCR and CSA `NA`, and remains in the fixed incident set; an unparseable/missing report has CFR, UCR, and CSA `NA` and a recorded failure. Any empty or failed report in either arm bars the **full confirmatory** RQ2 claim, even if a pooled rate from other incidents is defined. No affected incident is silently removed. The only pre-test exclusion is an incident failing independently documented eligibility or the paired-fork/leakage audit **before** predictions are inspected, with counts and reasons published. The following metric-specific rules further constrain this policy.

### 6.1. Three-class verifier outcomes

For label `k` in `{S,C,I}`, `TP_k`, `FP_k`, and `FN_k` come from the pooled confusion matrix on `P`. Every pair has one gold label and one predicted label, even if it was routed to review.

**Macro-F1 (RQ1 primary; RQ3 primary).** `F1_k = 2 TP_k / (2 TP_k + FP_k + FN_k)`; `MacroF1 = (F1_S + F1_C + F1_I)/3`. Numerator/denominator for each class are `2 TP_k` and `2 TP_k + FP_k + FN_k`. **Unit:** claim–evidence pair; **aggregation:** pooled confusion counts, then equal weight across the three classes. **Interpretation:** larger is better across all three labels. **Undefined:** if a class has no gold positives and no predictions, its class denominator is zero and Macro-F1 is `NA` for that stratum; report the other class scores and prevalence. Do not silently drop the absent class or change the class set.

**Supported Precision (RQ1 and RQ3 required joint endpoint).** `SP = TP_S / (TP_S + FP_S)` where numerator is gold Supported predicted Supported and denominator is every predicted Supported pair. **Unit:** claim–evidence pair; **aggregation:** pooled counts. **Interpretation:** larger means a Supported verdict is more trustworthy; it does not measure missed supported claims. **Undefined:** `NA` when no pair is predicted Supported; report that count and Supported recall separately.

**False Support Rate (RQ1 required joint endpoint; RQ3 mandatory descriptive safety diagnostic).** Proposed operationalization: `FSR = N(gold in {C,I} and predicted S) / N(gold in {C,I})`. **Unit:** claim–evidence pair; **aggregation:** pooled counts. **Interpretation:** smaller means fewer non-supported gold pairs receive a Supported verdict. **Undefined:** `NA` if no gold C/I pair exists. This denominator is distinct from Supported Precision's predicted-S denominator and must be approved before final evaluation; report false-support counts, C-to-S and I-to-S components, denominator, and uncertainty separately for each arm and environment. A zero numerator or degenerate interval cannot establish FSR superiority or non-inferiority.

**Supported Recall (safety diagnostic).** `SR = TP_S / (TP_S + FN_S)` from the same fixed `P`; pool pair counts. It is `NA` with no gold S pairs. Report it beside SP and review routing to expose blanket Insufficient predictions.

### 6.2. Citation and summary outcomes

**Citation Validity (RQ1 diagnostic; RQ2 secondary).** Proposed operationalization: `CV = N(valid in-scope citation references) / N(all emitted citation references)`. A reference is one claim–evidence-ID occurrence; repeated IDs on separate claims are separate occurrences. Validity requires the ID to resolve to an existing evidence record within the same permitted incident and tenant. **Unit:** emitted citation reference; **aggregation:** pooled counts. **Interpretation:** larger means references are resolvable and in scope, **not** that cited content supports a claim. **Undefined:** `NA` if no citations are emitted; additionally report the count of uncited factual claims so citation avoidance cannot appear successful.

**Citation Support Accuracy, CSA (RQ2 key secondary; RQ1 diagnostic).** `CSA = N(fully supported cited factual claims) / N(cited factual claims)`. A claim enters the denominator when it is factual and has at least one citation. The numerator requires its cited, in-scope evidence **as a permitted combination** to support every factual atom; an invalid, out-of-scope, or irrelevant extra citation cannot count as correct support. **Unit:** atomic factual claim with citations; **aggregation:** pooled counts. **Interpretation:** larger means cited claims are actually supported; it is not citation validity. **Undefined:** `NA` if there are no cited factual claims; report uncited factual claims and invalid references separately. The exact relevance rubric for an extra citation is pending W1-04.

**Unsupported Claim Rate, UCR (RQ2 co-primary safety outcome; RQ1 system diagnostic).** `UCR(a) = N(claims in C_a adjudicated Contradicted or Insufficient) / |C_a|`. Assess each factual claim against permitted incident evidence, whether cited or not. **Unit:** emitted atomic factual claim; **aggregation:** pooled counts across the fixed incidents for each arm. **Interpretation:** smaller is better; both direct contradiction and insufficient support are unsafe as factual assertions. The approved methodological `delta=0.03` is a maximum tolerated **three-percentage-point increase in this pooled rate**, conditional on the predeclared upper confidence bound for `UCR(S3)-UCR(S2)` being strictly below the approved margin; it does not bound the absolute number of unsupported claims because the arms can emit different numbers of claims. **Undefined:** `NA` if an arm emits no factual claims; report empty summaries and abstentions, not a favorable zero UCR. Truthful uncertainty statements are excluded by the locked claim-type rubric, not by post-hoc model preference.

**UCR dilution diagnostics (mandatory with RQ2).** For each incident and arm report `unsupported_count_i = N(C/I factual claims)`, `factual_count_i = |C_i|`, and `uncited_count_i = N(factual claims without a citation)`; give totals and equal-incident mean/median over valid reports, with zero for a valid empty report and `NA` for an unparseable report, plus the attempted/valid counts. `introduced_unsupported_i` counts S3 C/I claims without a semantically equivalent S2 C/I predecessor in the archived fork state; W1-04 must lock the equivalence rubric, and ambiguous matches are reported separately. Report count differences, claim/token length, and the number of incidents with an increase. These do not replace the predeclared pooled UCR gate. If counts rise despite a passing pooled-rate gate, state that the rate met the approved research margin while more unsupported assertions were observed; report their incident distribution and introduced-claim count, and do not claim an unqualified safety improvement. A failed or undefined UCR bound leaves the full RQ2 claim unsupported, even if CFR improves; CFR may be reported only as a component result.

**Critical Fact Recall, CFR (RQ2 primary coverage outcome).** `CFR(a) = N(eligible facts in F faithfully covered by arm a) / |F|`. A fact is eligible when the independent annotation identifies it as critical, Supported, and observable from the permitted evidence; low-criticality facts are not mandatory under the source plan. A semantically equivalent, correctly qualified mention may count only under the predeclared matching rubric; an unsupported mention does not count. **Unit:** eligible gold fact; **aggregation:** pooled counts across the same E2 incidents. **Interpretation:** larger means fewer supported critical facts were omitted. **Undefined:** `NA` if there are no eligible gold facts. Report fact eligibility and adjudication counts by incident. The exact criticality and semantic-match rubrics remain pending W1-04.

**Omission Rate (RQ2 secondary, derived).** `OR(a) = N(eligible facts not faithfully covered) / |F| = 1 - CFR(a)` using the **same** eligible facts and matching decisions. **Unit:** eligible gold fact; **aggregation:** pooled counts. **Interpretation:** smaller is better. **Undefined:** `NA` whenever `CFR` is `NA`; do not use a different denominator.

### 6.3. Cross-environment outcome

**OOD drops and advantages (RQ3).** For arm `a`, `Drop_F1(a) = MacroF1_A(a) - MacroF1_B(a)` and `Drop_SP(a) = SP_A(a) - SP_B(a)`; `A_F1 = Drop_F1(V2) - Drop_F1(V3)` and `A_SP = Drop_SP(V2) - Drop_SP(V3)`. Each environment metric uses Section 6.1 pooled counts on its fixed draft-claim pair set, followed by these arithmetic differences. **Unit:** claim–evidence pair within environment. **Interpretation:** a positive drop is degradation and a positive advantage means V3 degrades less. Negative drops are reported, not clipped. **Undefined:** if an environment lacks either component metric, its associated drop/advantage is `NA`; A and B are never falsely paired. Only `A_F1` and `A_SP` are confirmatory RQ3 endpoints. Always report absolute B F1, SP, FSR, false-support numerators/denominators, uncertainty, class prevalence, and A scores alongside relative drops; no FSR non-inferiority or absolute performance threshold is implied. Report `FSR_B(V3)-FSR_B(V2)` descriptively and flag observed worsening without turning a zero-event or degenerate interval into a safety claim.

### 6.4. Secondary metric dictionary (approved methodology; implementation pending)

All denominators below use the predeclared attempted population, eligible subset, or successful-measurement subset as stated; report the count of each. A measurement marked `NA` is never silently zero-filled. Report per-environment values separately where relevant.

| Outcome | Numerator / denominator or statistic; unit and aggregation | Undefined and failure rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retrieval Recall@`k` (`k=5,10`) | For incident `i` with human-adjudicated supporting evidence IDs `G_i`, `|G_i ∩ TopK_i| / |G_i|`; mean over **completed** eligible incidents, with deduplicated ranked IDs and deterministic tie rule fixed in configuration. | `NA` for an incident with `|G_i|=0` or unavailable retrieval; report each reason, attempted count and completed eligible denominator. |

## 7. Dataset and evaluation policy

The source plan's **aspirational target**, not an observed dataset, is 80 incident bundles: 40 train, 10 validation, 15 in-domain test, and 15 OOD test. Its fallback is 50–60 incidents with a distinct OOD test and an explicitly described feasibility limitation; an exact fallback allocation is OPEN. No dataset availability, license, collected count, or annotation count is certified by this protocol. W1-05 must document legal source use and an independent candidate Environment B before collection claims are made.

Assign immutable IDs and provenance to incident bundles, source events, evidence snapshots, human annotations, operational facts, and derived perturbations. Event provenance includes source, collection method, timestamp, raw-message reference, and hash; a hash demonstrates snapshot integrity, not that the original event was truthful. Preserve raw events. Every retrieval, citation, and fact-scoring step must enforce incident/tenant scope.

**Grouping and isolation.** Assign immutable parent/descendant links for original incident, evidence snapshots, claim mutations, counterfactuals, perturbations, and generated derivatives. Build a graph connecting records that share an original incident, simulation run, concrete template lineage, or source-collection lineage capable of reproducing the same scenario; where environment or source collection defines the intended holdout, keep the entire relevant collection/environment out of train and validation. Split **connected components**, never individual records. All descendants inherit the parent's partition; synthetic claim mutations and counterfactual supervision are **train only**. Before freeze, enumerate cross-partition duplicates and near-duplicates of incident IDs, run/template IDs, source event hashes, evidence text, claim text, and prompts. Record reviewer, audit method, matches, and dispositions in the manifest. A component with unresolved overlap is quarantined from confirmatory test/OOD, not merely disclosed. If separation cannot be achieved at the planned sample size, downgrade the affected comparison to exploratory and publish the overlap. Use validation, not test/OOD labels, for checkpoint, threshold, rule, retrieval, prompt, and stopping-policy selection. W1-07 owns exact group-key definitions, split IDs, checksum, and freeze; W1-05 supplies source lineage.

**Gold annotation.** Test and OOD labels are human-reviewed. At least a documented subset is independently annotated by two annotators, with disagreement, reason codes, adjudicator, and resolution retained. Report actual double-annotation counts and agreement with a method chosen before results are inspected; do not imply that an agreement score exists yet. Gold critical facts must come from independent review of source evidence/timelines, not from generator outputs or S3's operational fact inventory. Output-claim support and fact-coverage adjudicators necessarily inspect reports, but must be blinded to S2/S3 arm identity and predictions under a locked rubric; maintain an audit of blinding breaches. W1-04 owns detailed S/C/I, criticality, observability, uncertainty, and semantic-match rubrics. Disputed or unobservable facts receive an explicit adjudication status and cannot be silently treated as false.

**Test protection.** Before final evaluation, version and checksum split manifests, arm-independent draft-generation configuration, frozen draft/evidence snapshots, pair-sampling manifest, gold labels, eligible-fact rules, model/prompt/config versions, thresholds, metric code, missing-output policy, statistical plan, and denominator policies. Restrict test/OOD access and retain an access/audit record. Verify the overlap audit, B-independence dossier, draft-generation independence from V2/V3, and gold-annotation blinding before unsealing; verify paired S2/S3 archive identity before scoring its outputs. No final-test examples or scores may be used to revise a confirmatory method without a versioned amendment, human approval, and a clearly labeled exploratory rerun. Report any leakage or protocol deviation even if it weakens a result.

## 8. Statistical analysis

Use **paired** V3/V2 predictions on the same E1/E3 claim-pair IDs and **paired** S3/S2 outputs from the same archived S2 state on the same E2 incident IDs. Report point estimates, absolute differences, class/incident/independent-cluster counts, confidence bounds, confusion matrices, per-environment scores, and an error taxonomy. Claim pairs and gold facts within an incident and incidents sharing a run/template lineage are correlated; none are independent bootstrap units.

**Lineage-cluster paired bootstrap (approved methodology; implementation pending).** The resampling unit is the largest independent connected component established by the W1-07 overlap audit: all incidents sharing original incident, simulation run, concrete template lineage, or scenario-producing collection stay together. A broad collection/environment that leaves only one such unit makes inference across collections impossible; do not split it merely to obtain a narrow interval. Resample these units with replacement **within** each evaluation environment/stratum, carrying all incidents, gold labels, claim pairs, facts, and both compared arms together. Recompute pooled metrics and paired differences from counts on every replicate. For RQ3, resample A and B independently while preserving V2/V3 pairing inside each environment, then recompute both drops and `A_F1`/`A_SP`. Use one recorded seed and the same indices for all arms and endpoints of a comparison. The proposed count is 10,000 replicates; report distinct-cluster counts, seed, replicate count, valid/invalid counts by endpoint, and empirical bound method. Compute descriptive B FSR and paired-difference intervals from the same within-B lineage resamples where defined, alongside counts/denominators; mark zero-event or degenerate intervals as uninformative about an FSR safety comparison, never as evidence of non-inferiority.

**Approved validity rule (calibration pending).** Use empirical 2.5th/97.5th percentiles for a two-sided 95% interval on RQ1 directional differences and the two RQ3 drop advantages; for RQ2 use the 5th percentile as a one-sided 95% lower bound on `Delta CFR` and the 95th percentile as a one-sided 95% upper bound on `Delta UCR`. Do not discard invalid replicates and silently renormalize: if a required confirmatory endpoint has any undefined observed estimate, fewer than 10 independent resampling units in a required stratum, or at least 5% invalid replicates, mark its interval `NA` and the corresponding RQ exploratory. These 10-unit/5%-invalid thresholds and 10,000 bootstrap replicates are **PROPOSED necessary implementation checks, not guarantees of interval coverage or type-I error control**. Report all invalid reasons, including absent classes and zero predicted-S denominators. For valid intervals, calculate percentiles on valid replicates and state their count. W1-07 must verify independent units before the lock; if collection-level dependence cannot be represented by enough units, restrict inference to the observed collections and make no general cross-collection claim. FSR in RQ3 remains descriptive regardless of interval validity; zero observed events do not establish a zero underlying risk.

**Pre-test CI calibration gate (approved methodology; execution pending).** W1-07 owns the pre-freeze audit of independent connected-component counts in each required A/B stratum and component size/source/family heterogeneity. A separate blinded data custodian under W1-07 audits final-test gold class availability, non-supported gold pairs needed for FSR, and eligible gold facts needed for CFR; the detailed label counts stay sealed and are not inputs to CI calibration or method selection. Validation/pilot data, not final-test predictions, labels, or reports, supply anticipated class prevalence, predicted-S and emitted-claim denominator risks. W7 (23–29/11) locks the statistical method, completes CI calibration and all mandatory pre-test audits, then, only if they pass, executes confirmatory evaluation on held-out A/B. Calibration uses only train/validation or separately designated pilot data and the non-label structural W1-07 design audit. Predeclare and version the simulation code, data-generating assumptions, seed, Monte Carlo precision/replicate count, scenario grid, endpoint definitions, and acceptance rule before running calibration. The custodian releases only eligibility pass/fail after that lock; failure downgrades the affected RQ rather than prompting method tuning. Pilot scores, including W3/W4 source-derived verifier scores, may inform stress scenarios but cannot be promoted to confirmatory RQ1/RQ3 results or used to select a method after final-test inspection.

**Calibration estimands and scenarios.** The nominal endpoint confidence level is 95%: two-sided intervals for RQ1/RQ3 and one-sided bounds for RQ2, as specified above. In independently simulated complete datasets with known true contrasts, *usable coverage* is the fraction in which the required interval/bound is defined and contains the true contrast (`lower <= truth <= upper`, `lower <= truth`, or `upper >= truth`, respectively). An undefined interval counts as noncoverage; also report coverage conditional on defined intervals and all undefined reasons. For a full conjunctive RQ claim, *Type I error* is the probability of falsely confirming all required H1 gates when at least one component of the composite null is true. A failed or undefined gate means only that H1 was **not established**, not that H1 was disproved. *Type II error* is failure to confirm full H1 when every component is truly favorable; report estimated power (`1 - Type II error`) in predeclared favorable scenarios, but do not use power to pass the Type I/coverage gate.

The calibration must replay the paired lineage-cluster resampling and complete gate logic, preserving within-component correlations and V2/V3 or S2/S3 pairing. W7 must freeze a small scenario grid from train/validation or designated pilot data and the non-label W1-07 structural audit **before** simulation; no final-test labels, predictions, scores, or prevalence may choose scenarios. At minimum include a design-matched scenario and adverse sparse-class/zero-denominator, unequal-cluster-size, stronger within-cluster dependence, and source/family-heterogeneity conditions; RQ3 scenarios keep A/B collections distinct. For Type I error, put each component null on its boundary in turn while other components are predeclared favorable, and include a joint-boundary check. RQ2 must include `Delta CFR = 0` with `Delta UCR < delta`, `Delta UCR = delta` with `Delta CFR > 0`, and their joint boundary. RQ1 has three component boundaries and RQ3 has two. Report endpoint usable/conditional coverage, full-claim Type I error, invalid/undefined frequency, and sensitivity across scenarios. These finite scenarios cannot guarantee coverage or Type I error for every real telemetry distribution.

**Approved calibration screening policy (execution and scenario lock pending).** Endpoint intervals/bounds target nominal 95% confidence, and the full conjunctive claim targets nominal 5% Type I error conditional on valid inference. The approved **screening tolerances** allow a two-percentage-point shortfall in usable coverage (minimum 93%) and a two-percentage-point excess in full-claim Type I error (maximum 7%); these are not nominal confidence or error guarantees. For every predeclared required scenario, require the one-sided 95% *exact-binomial Monte Carlo lower bound* for each required endpoint's usable coverage to be at least 93%. In each **composite-null** scenario, also require the one-sided 95% *exact-binomial Monte Carlo upper bound* for false full-H1 confirmation to be at most 7%; this Type I criterion does not apply to true-H1 power scenarios. Independently generate a **fixed initial budget of 1,000 simulated datasets per scenario**, with the same proposed **10,000 inner bootstrap resamples per dataset** as the final-evaluation procedure; record seeds, scenario parameters, successes, failures, bounds, and undefined cases. At a true probability of 95% or 5%, 1,000 independent outer simulations have an approximate Monte Carlo standard error of 0.69 percentage points. Evaluate the predeclared bounds once after all 1,000 datasets; a failed or inconclusive bound, including one whose point estimate meets the tolerance, or a non-estimable required scenario fails the affected RQ's calibration gate and prevents its confirmatory conclusion. **No optional or outcome-triggered top-up is authorized.** Any sequential extension requires a separately approved, statistically valid pre-test procedure; changing scenarios, methods, or thresholds after inspecting calibration results also requires a versioned pre-test amendment and human approval. Passing screens only the predeclared scenarios for material miscalibration; it does **not** establish strict 95% coverage, 5% Type I control, operational safety, or validity under unmodeled shifts. W7 must lock the non-test scenario grid and implementation details before calibration and final-test access.

**W7 feasibility and fallback.** Outer simulated datasets are distinct from the 10,000 inner bootstrap resamples used to construct each simulated interval and proposed for final evaluation. The minimum is 10 million low-dimensional count reaggregations **per scenario**; a seven-boundary grid plus favorable/power scenarios can require over 100 million. W7 should reuse cached incident/lineage count arrays, avoid LLM/model calls inside the simulation, and benchmark runtime and memory on non-test synthetic counts before committing to the pre-test lock; no runtime or completed calibration is claimed here. If the approved grid cannot be completed within available resources, the method fails calibration, independent clusters are insufficient, a required class/denominator is absent, or a locked final-test interval is undefined, do not issue the affected confirmatory RQ claim. Report fixed-denominator estimates and limitations as exploratory/feasibility. An alternative CI method or run budget requires a versioned **pre-test** amendment and human approval; never select it from final-test results.

**RQ2 non-inferiority and multiplicity.** Let `Delta UCR = UCR(S3) - UCR(S2)` on the fixed incidents. The approved methodological **pooled-rate** margin `delta = 0.03` (three percentage points) is not an allowance of three additional unsupported claims per 100 emitted claims or a cap on the absolute unsupported count. **Research-design rationale:** three percentage points is the source plan's pre-test, finite tolerance for a possible pooled unsupported-assertion rate increase while testing whether omission repair improves critical-fact coverage. It makes the permissible rate trade-off explicit and testable instead of treating a nonsignificant difference as safety. It is a normative design choice about analyst-facing error tolerance, **not an empirically validated SOC safety threshold, evidence that three points is harmless, or a guarantee about absolute unsupported counts**; no empirical justification for that exact value exists yet. The project owner approved this research tolerance and mandatory count and length diagnostics on 2026-10-13; W7 must still lock and implement it before final-test access. A higher absolute unsupported count after S3 must be disclosed and qualify any passed rate-level conclusion under Section 6.2. The full RQ2 claim is an intersection–union test: require the one-sided 95% lower bound for `Delta CFR` > 0 and the one-sided 95% upper bound for `Delta UCR` < approved delta. Both gates must pass on the predeclared pooled metrics; no multiplicity adjustment is needed for this single conjunctive claim **at nominal alpha 0.05, conditional on valid bounds**. RQ1 and RQ3 also use conjunctive gate claims at nominal alpha no greater than 0.05 under valid endpoint intervals; RQ3's two advantage gates support relative robustness only, with FSR reported descriptively. The approved calibration screening tolerances above do not prove exact finite-sample alpha control. Three separately reported RQ claims are **not** advertised as a familywise-controlled set of discoveries. A global claim that all three RQs succeeded requires all three full gates and explicit disclosure of any RQ3 FSR worsening; it cannot imply FSR safety or operational readiness. Exploratory ablations/slices receive no confirmatory p-value claim. If a separately familywise-controlled claim about *any one* RQ is desired, require a pre-test multiplicity amendment and approval. A failed or undefined UCR bound precludes the full RQ2 non-inferiority claim regardless of CFR; “no statistically significant difference” is insufficient.

**Interpretation.** Separate predeclared primary comparisons from V0/V1 context, S0/S1 context, ablations, perturbations, optional fine-tuning, and post-hoc error slices. Do not promote a favorable secondary analysis to a primary finding. If sample size or environment independence is inadequate, label conclusions exploratory/feasibility and report negative and indeterminate findings. No observed effect, confidence interval, power, or generalization claim is available from this methodological specification.
