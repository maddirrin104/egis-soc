## W1-07 lineage and sealed-access audit

W1-07 must maintain a **private structural graph** with nodes for original incident, concrete execution run, concrete template and parameterization, source collection, source event, immutable raw snapshot, all derived snapshots and descendant artifacts. Edges include `same_original_incident`, `same_run`, `uses_template`, `collected_in`, `contains_event`, `copied_from`, `transformed_from`, and `shares_parent_dataset`, with evidence URL/ID/hash, reviewer and uncertainty. The identity fields and raw hashes are for de-duplication and traceability; a digest alone does not prove ownership or independence. Compare connected components across **train, validation, held-out A and held-out B**; unresolved cross-partition links are quarantined, not waived by a hostname/schema change. Record the original collection and source-family relationship even when individual events differ. A shared broad ATT&CK technique alone need not connect clusters, while the same concrete scenario template/run, source event, copied snapshot or parent collection demands explicit overlap review under the [protocol](../research-protocol.md) §7.

The custodian performs this audit in a restricted workspace. The development team submits A-side lineage keys and a fixed comparison rule; the custodian runs comparisons against sealed B structural metadata and releases only a documented **pass/fail/unknown**, reasons stated at a non-sensitive category level, and a signed audit reference. No B raw event, gold label, class breakdown, score, threshold, exact held-out ID/manifest, per-item result or cluster count is released for model selection or calibration. The approved protocol's blinded class/denominator eligibility check remains a separate, later pass/fail gate after method lock. Auditors may inspect what is needed under access control; their findings must not feed development. Check access logs and a seal manifest before W7 access. Independent review of lineage and environment independence is required before final-test access; W1-05 metadata review does not satisfy it.

**Execution order:** W6 may complete source-rights review, selective acquisition, private graph construction, independence audit, source profiling by the custodian, and train/validation or designated **non-B** pilot experiments. W7 first locks model/checkpoint, evidence retrieval, prompts, thresholds, scoring, claim-pair generation and split/lineage manifests using permitted development material; performs approved non-test statistical calibration and required eligibility/access audits; then, only if all gates pass, runs sealed held-out A/B evaluation.
