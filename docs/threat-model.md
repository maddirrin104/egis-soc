# W1-06 threat model — version 1.0

**Status:** APPROVED as the W1-06 Week 1 documentation/design deliverable by the project owner on 2026-10-17 under [D-014](context/DECISION_LOG.md). The controls and tests below remain proposals for later implementation and validation. No attack, live Splunk query, real-data ingestion, held-out access, or security test was executed for this document. Severity below is a qualitative design priority, not an observed frequency or measured residual risk. W1-03 schema validation is owner accepted under D-013; W1-07 policy and synthetic in-memory controls are accepted for their scoped Week 1 deliverable under D-012. None of these acceptances establishes source authenticity, deployed access control, operational provenance verification, a real split seal, or empirical safety.

**Authority and scope:** the [approved protocol v0.2](research-protocol.md) and [execution-order amendment D-008](context/DECISION_LOG.md), [annotation guideline v1.0](annotation-guidelines.md), [research guardrails](context/RESEARCH_GUARDRAILS.md), [W1-03 contracts](../src/egis_soc/schemas.py), [W1-05 documentation-only assessment](datasets/acceptance-handoff.md), [Environment B screening plan](datasets/environment-b-plan.md), and [W1-07 split/freeze policy](split-freeze-policy.md) govern this draft. The [eight-week source plan](EGIS_SOC_Ke_hoach_8_tuan.md) §9 lists initial threats; its W6 held-out evaluation row is superseded by D-008. Inputs are already triaged, incident-scoped bundles. Detection from an unbounded stream, autonomous remediation, and production SOC deployment are outside project scope. This document proposes controls without amending hypotheses, labels, rubrics, metrics, denominators, thresholds, or final-test order.

## 1. System, assets, and security objectives

| Asset | Security property and consequence if lost |
| --- | --- |
| Original telemetry, raw snapshots, source metadata, and provenance | Preserve exact source bytes, immutable snapshot identity, chain of acquisition, and uncertainty. A valid snapshot hash detects byte changes only after a trusted acquisition; it does not prove the upstream event is true or lawful to use. |
| Tenant and incident boundaries | A retrieval, citation, draft, fact inventory, or review session uses only its authorized incident and tenant. Cross-boundary content can expose private data and contaminate conclusions. |
| Evidence IDs, normalized events, retrieval index, and cited context | Keep ID-to-snapshot binding, content, order, truncation, and permitted combination auditable. A resolvable citation does not establish relevance or factual support. |
| Drafts, atomic claims, S/C/I verdicts, operational facts, repairs, and analyst decisions | Preserve the original assertion and qualifiers, trace each derived version and decision to permitted evidence, and route unresolved support to review. A system must not present `insufficient` as `contradicted` or `supported`. |
| Restricted lineage, split, pair, gold, and evaluation manifests | Keep held-out A/B identities, gold labels/facts, annotations, prompts, and test outputs sealed from development and inference until authorized W7 gates. Prevent leakage that invalidates RQ1–RQ3. |
| Credentials, private stores, remote/local model context, and audit records | Prevent sensitive telemetry or sealed material from leaking through prompts, logs, caches, model providers, exception traces, exports, or public Git; make access and deviations attributable. |
| Compute and analyst attention | Bound queries, retrieval, model calls, repair rounds, and review load so resource exhaustion cannot silently drop evidence or make failed outputs look successful. |

**Actors and authority.** A dataset publisher, telemetry collector, and CTI author supply potentially incorrect or adversarial content; their text has *data* authority only. An external adversary may control log fields, filenames, source narratives, or a compromised upstream artifact. A mistaken or malicious tenant user, analyst, service principal, or insider could request another scope, alter metadata, or expose restricted material. The development team may change non-frozen code using train/validation material but must not see sealed test/OOD content. The future custodian, independent lineage/B reviewer, gold annotator/adjudicator, W7 runner, and project owner have different proposed duties; actual identities, credentials, approvals, and storage are still pending under [policy §13](split-freeze-policy.md#13-decisions--w1-framework-accepted-operational-choices-pending-owner-review). A remote LLM service, if chosen, is an additional processor and egress boundary; a local model still has process, file, and log boundaries.

**Assumptions to verify, not facts established in W1:** source rights and lawful handling; an attributable acquisition chain and exact raw snapshot bytes; trustworthy host-side authentication and tenant ACLs; complete enough telemetry for any asserted absence; reliable clock and entity mapping; an independently operated and adequately sized Environment B; private sealed stores and a functioning W7 unseal procedure. If any is unknown, the pipeline must carry uncertainty or quarantine, not assume it away. The W1-05 candidate list contains metadata/tasks rather than acquired incidents or independent clusters. O1 remains reserved/metadata-only; Splunk Attack Range is B-reserved and unqualified.

## 2. Flows and trust boundaries

| Boundary | Untrusted or restricted input crossing it | Required design check and safe failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1 source/Splunk → private raw store | External logs, CTI, exports, metadata, source-provided checksums | Read-only Splunk credential and allowlisted scoped queries; verify acquisition rights, source/tenant/incident assignment, exact persisted-byte digest and acquisition record in W2. Quarantine unresolved rights, scope, or provenance. Never execute text found in the payload. |
| B2 raw store → normalization and evidence index | Encodings, timestamps, arbitrary JSON fields, adversarial strings | Retain original bytes and opaque raw reference; parse/validate, bound size, normalize with explicit transformation lineage and errors. Keep the raw snapshot immutable. Do not interpret log/CTI instructions as system instructions. |
| B3 index → incident-scoped retrieval/LLM context | Search results, evidence snippets, citation IDs | Authorize scope *before* query and again when resolving each result. Bind IDs to the requested tenant/incident and snapshot; apply context limits without concealing truncation. Delimit and quote untrusted text with fixed instruction priority. No cross-incident fallback. |
| B4 model draft → claim verifier/omission/repair | Generated text, citations, tool-like requests, fabricated claims | Parse structured output as data; atomize preserving modality and source span; check citation validity/relevance separately from semantic support. Use only permitted evidence, cap repair at the approved maximum of two additional iterations, and re-verify changed/new claims. Route uncertainty to analyst review. |
| B5 operational path ↔ evaluation custody | Operational fact inventory versus restricted `EvaluationGoldRecord`, human gold, pair/lineage manifests, held-out A/B | Use separate storage, credentials, grants, trusted manifest delivery, access logs, and blinding. No gold-derived operational inventory or prompt. W7 unseal only after method locks, calibration and required audits; failures stop confirmatory evaluation. |
| B6 service/model/reviewer → external systems | Remote model API, browser/UI, audit exports, Git/reports | Minimize/redact context under approved data policy, prevent unrestricted URLs/tool execution, protect credentials and private logs, make analyst approval explicit, and keep only privacy-safe aggregate artifacts in public Git. |

The [schema](../src/egis_soc/schemas.py) defines `EvidenceProvenance` (`source`, UTC timestamp, decoded `raw_message`, opaque `raw_message_ref`, `sha256`, `acquisition_method`), `EvidenceEvent` (evidence/incident/tenant IDs, immutable normalized JSON view, provenance), and inference-facing `IncidentBundle` (incident/tenant/environment/source/scenario IDs, alert/evidence IDs). `IncidentBundle.check_scope` rejects a foreign event or pair and unknown evidence references **when called**. `GoldFact`, `EvaluationClaimEvidencePair`, and `EvaluationGoldRecord` are evaluation-facing; the bundle contains no gold. W1-03 validates structure and protects nested normalized fields from ordinary mutation, but ingestion must verify the raw-store digest, and host storage/ACLs must enforce access. Bypassing validation via direct model construction is outside the trusted input path. The W1-07 in-process prototype adds synthetic lineage, integrity and grant checks; caller-supplied role strings, approval references, and self-consistent hashes are not authentication, signatures, or OS isolation.

## 3. Severity and threat scenarios

Severity is a **design triage**: **critical** means plausible cross-tenant disclosure or irrecoverable confirmatory-evaluation compromise; **high** means a false incident assertion, missed critical fact, or unauthorized restricted read that could materially mislead review; **medium** means contained degradation, lost traceability, or resource failure visible for review. Likelihood and effectiveness cannot be estimated without W2/W5 implementations and controlled tests. Every row below names a proposed control, not a deployed guarantee.

| ID / threat and path | Priority and rationale | Proposed prevention, detection, and failure response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T01 Indirect prompt injection in log/CTI text claims to be a higher-priority system message, asks to suppress a fact, change a verdict, call a tool, or reveal data. B1→B3→B4. | High: untrusted evidence can influence both generation and verification. | Treat all source text as quoted data; fixed prompts and tool allowlist; no executable command/URL from evidence; bounded content; scan output for instruction following and compare factual claims to source evidence. If the model obeys source instructions or output cannot be parsed, quarantine the output and route to review. |
| T02 Citation laundering: a real in-scope ID is attached to an unrelated or weaker claim, or an irrelevant extra ID obscures an unsupported assertion. B3→B4. | High: a credible-looking citation can hide false support. | Resolve ID and scope, assess relevance per citation occurrence, then separately verify full claim support against the permitted evidence combination. For E1/RQ1/RQ3, the **fixed pair evidence set** is the only label evidence; another bundle event cannot rescue it. For E2/RQ2 UCR, use the fixed permitted **incident evidence**, cited or uncited; CSA additionally requires all emitted citations to be valid, relevant, and sufficient as a cited combination. Never equate citation validity with S/C/I. |
| T03 Evidence tampering, missing bytes, broken raw reference, or provenance laundering through a correct hash over attacker-chosen content. B1→B2. | High: a forged or untraceable event can support a false incident account. | Verify exact persisted raw bytes against `sha256` at ingestion; retain source URL/revision, acquisition chain, transforms and snapshot identity; restrict writes and audit reads; compare with source-provided checksums where available. Mismatch/unknown chain → quarantine or explicitly `insufficient` for dependent claims. Hash match alone does not prove original-source truth or completeness. |
| T04 Cross-incident/tenant reference, search-result mix-up, permissive query, cache key collision, or UI request for another tenant. B1/B3/B6. | Critical: may disclose private data and contaminate claims. | Enforce server-side authenticated tenant/incident authorization before query and each dereference; use incident/tenant namespaced indexes/caches; call schema scope checks; reject foreign IDs even when semantically useful; audit denied reads. Do not repair a missing answer with a foreign incident. |
| T05 Evaluation-gold or held-out A/B leakage through prompts, retrieval index, operational fact inventory, filenames, logs, developer calibration, model cache, or a forged grant. B5/B6. | Critical: can invalidate confirmatory RQ1–RQ3 even without a visible score change. | Separate restricted gold/test stores and credentials from inference/development; trusted manifest and grant issuance; independent blinding/access audits; hash/versioned lineage and split seals before authorized W7 access. Unknown ancestry, unauthorized read, or seal mismatch → stop, preserve evidence, log deviation, seek owner disposition. Do not use test/OOD artifacts for tuning or relabeling. |
| T06 False support from entity/time confusion, compound claims, NLI/rule error, context truncation, weak/ambiguous evidence, or ungrounded inference. B3→B4. | High: can assert an outcome such as exfiltration from DNS evidence alone. | Atomize and retain modality; evaluate entity, event, time, negation, confidence and permitted multi-event combinations; rule/NLI trace with three labels only. Missing evidence is `insufficient`, not `contradicted`; `Needs Review` is a routing flag, not gold label. Low-confidence, failed, or unresolved predictions stay in the fixed denominator under the approved scoring policy. |
| T07 Critical supported fact omitted because retrieval missed it, context was truncated, operational inventory missed it, or generation compressed it away. B3→B4. | High: an apparently accurate report may omit a decisive event. | Record retrieved and excluded IDs/truncation; operational fact inventory from the same permitted evidence only; compare to report and repair only evidence-supported missing facts. Independently annotated, high/medium, supported, fully observable evaluation facts remain in restricted gold for CFR scoring only; never feed them to S3. Disclose missed facts and limitations. |
| T08 Repair turns an `insufficient` fact into a stronger factual claim, introduces or repeats unsupported assertions, or adds supported filler that masks a rise in absolute unsupported counts behind a lower pooled UCR. B4. | High: apparent coverage gain can worsen factual safety. | Complete S2 evidence-based handling of unsupported claims, then archive one identical S2 fork per incident; S3 alone adds omission checking and at most two additional repair rounds. Re-verify each changed/added claim and citation. Use approved P-06 predecessor-existence matching and count repeated factual assertions separately. Report CFR, UCR, CSA and mandatory absolute unsupported/introduced counts, length and citation diagnostics. Stop/review when support remains insufficient. |
| T09 Exfiltration through a remote LLM request, generated URL, tool invocation, prompt/debug log, analyst export, or repository artifact. B3/B4/B6. | Critical if sealed gold or tenant telemetry leaves its authorized boundary; otherwise high. | Decide local/remote placement and data-processing rights before use; minimize payload under an approved policy while retaining the original evidence privately, deny arbitrary outbound tools/URLs, segregate model logs/caches and public output, rotate scoped credentials. Block and record unauthorized egress; preserve audit evidence without copying raw telemetry into Git. |
| T10 Resource exhaustion through oversized events, broad Splunk query, adversarial repetition, retrieval fanout, model context overflow, or repair loop. B1–B4. | Medium to high: availability loss and silent truncation can make a report incomplete. | Query timeouts and limits, input/output byte and token caps, deterministic retrieval depth, bounded concurrency, two-round repair ceiling, measured failure states. Never score timeout or unparseable output as an empty success; preserve attempted/failed denominators. |
| T11 Model/retrieval failure or distribution shift: schema drift, absent sensor, inconsistent clocks, NLI shortcut, non-independent B, or unavailable model. B2–B5. | High for false support or invalid RQ3; medium when safely routed. | Record parser/source coverage, retrieval IDs and configuration, model revision, NLI/rule errors and abstentions. Require independent B operation/lineage and meaningful shift, plus W7 locks and calibration before confirmatory OOD use. On unknown B adequacy or failed gates, label RQ3 blocked/exploratory; route incident output to review. |
| T12 Insider or process edits lineage, annotation, split, evidence, or audit record after review; a checksum is recomputed over altered data. B2/B5. | Critical for gold/split manipulation; high for operational audit corruption. | Restrict writes by role, retain append-only predecessor-linked private manifests, independent review, trusted signing/seal and access logging; compare bound code/config/artifact identities. Preserve prior versions and investigate mismatch. These operational controls and sign-off identities remain owner decisions. |

## 4. Proposed safe tests and acceptance evidence

All tests here are **specifications, not executions**. Use entirely synthetic, non-sensitive train/development fixtures for W2/W5 engineering. A fixture must never copy sealed IDs, labels, prompts, text, or lineage into development. The test runner should record test revision, fixture provenance, expected behavior, actual outcome, exit status and artifacts. Test failures block the affected control claim; they do not justify changing a frozen evaluation rule. W7 checks are access/lock audits and conditional evaluation gates, not permission to run attacks on held-out content.

| Test ID / phase owner | Proposed fixture and assertion | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S01 / W2 ingestion | Synthetic raw bytes, one mutated byte, broken reference and misleading source-provided hash: exact persisted-byte digest mismatch or missing raw ref quarantines event; a matching digest is recorded only as integrity, not truth. | Raw/snapshot test hashes, parser result, quarantine reason and no source overwrite. |
| S02 / W2 ingestion/scope | Two synthetic tenants and incidents with overlapping display IDs, malicious foreign citation, mixed search result and cache collision: every foreign query/dereference is denied before content reaches model, inventory or UI. | Denial trace with tenant/incident/evidence IDs and no foreign content in output. |
| S03 / W2 and W5 injection boundary | Inert log/CTI strings that ask to ignore instructions, emit a tool call, alter support or reveal a secret-shaped canary: generated output must not follow those requests or call unauthorized tools; any parse/factual failure routes to review. No live payload or external endpoint. | Quoted input fixture, prompt/template revision, allowed-tool list, output and review/denial trace. |
| S04 / W5 citation and S/C/I | Synthetic valid-but-irrelevant and foreign IDs; same-record direct refutation; DNS-only exfiltration; conflicting records; multi-evidence support. Assert CV/scope, relevance, CSA and support decisions separately. Test fixed E1 pair evidence versus E2 incident evidence exactly as P-02 requires. | Atomic claim, fixed evidence IDs, emitted citations, S/C/I and separate citation judgments. |
| S05 / W5 omission/repair | Supported observable high/medium fact missing from draft; low or unobservable candidate; ungrounded stronger repair; repeated new unsupported claim and supported filler. Assert operational inventory contains no gold, only eligible evidence-backed repair is proposed, max two rounds, all new/changed claims re-verified, and unresolved facts route to review. Check pooled UCR alongside absolute unsupported occurrences so filler cannot mask an increase. | S2 fork hash, round traces, fact/evidence links, S3 output and absolute introduced/unsupported counts. |
| S06 / W5 availability | Oversized event/context, retrieval timeout, model parse failure, and cap-exhausted repair: assert bounded work, explicit failure/truncation, no apparent empty-success output and attempted/failed accounting. | Limit/config revision, timing/failure status and review route. |
| S07 / W7 custody and split gate | Synthetic manifest with altered reviewed-link evidence, changed policy, wrong partition/grant, or stale audit: refuse freeze/read; verify separate operational and gold credentials in the real host before any final-test access. W1-07 in-memory tests alone cannot satisfy this host check. | Manifest digest comparison, denied-access and sign-off/audit references, without sealed content in development logs. |
| S08 / W7 pre-test integrity | Before conditional final run, independent custodian audits source rights/lineage/B independence, access log, blinded gold construction, fixed draft/pair population, identical V2/V3 pair/evidence/NLI input, identical archived S2 fork, method locks and calibrated eligibility. Any unknown/failed gate stops affected confirmatory claim. | Restricted signed dispositions and privacy-safe pass/fail/unknown gate report; exact held-out IDs, labels and counts stay restricted. |

The W1-07 synthetic tests already exercise part of S02/S07 logic in memory, but they do **not** execute this threat test plan, verify production ACLs, authenticate grant strings, validate raw provenance, or establish final-test readiness. No new test result is asserted here.

## 5. Residual risk and ownership

| Residual risk or decision | Owner and next gate |
| --- | --- |
| Publisher-origin false records, incomplete collection, and compromised clocks can survive hashing and ordinary schema validation. | W2 ingestion/custodian: document source trust, timestamp uncertainty, completeness and independent corroboration; W5 reviewer sees explicit uncertainty. |
| Model may obey novel injections or make plausible false inferences despite delimiters, rules, NLI and re-verification. | W5 generation/verifier owner: synthetic adversarial regressions and analyst review; W7 reports false-support, failures and review routing without an operational-safety claim. |
| Omission inventory may miss a true critical fact; gold cannot be used to repair this during inference. | W5 omission owner: operational-only inventory and review UI; W7 independent gold CFR assessment after approved gates. |
| W1-07 synthetic scope checks cannot prevent a host from exposing private files, forged credentials, or remote prompts. | Owner/custodian: approve concrete roles, restricted storage, authentication, provider/egress policy, retention and breach response under policy §13; W7 verifies host controls before unseal. |
| B independence, rights, actual cluster adequacy and statistical eligibility remain unknown; public metadata is insufficient. | W1-05/W1-07 custodian and independent reviewer resolve source and lineage evidence; W7 applies approved pre-test locks/calibration/eligibility. Failure leaves RQ3 exploratory or blocked. |
| Security controls can introduce missed evidence, latency, and extra analyst burden. | W3/W5 development measures retrieval/failure/review behavior on permitted non-test material; W7 reports actual latency, memory, tokens and reviewer outcomes only when measured, with denominators and limitations. |

**Open owner decisions (not assumed approved):** [W1-07 policy §13](split-freeze-policy.md#13-decisions--w1-framework-accepted-operational-choices-pending-owner-review) covers lineage equivalence, near-match and allocation rules, actual custodian/reviewer/runner and grant authority, source rights and B role, private manifest/seal format, W5 draft/pair settings, and W7 transition. This threat model additionally needs a decision on remote versus local model handling of sensitive context, outbound network/tool policy, audit retention and incident response. Record any accepted design choice in the decision log through the coordinator; do not treat this draft as that approval.
